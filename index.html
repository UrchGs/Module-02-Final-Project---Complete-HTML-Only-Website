Maths tutoring that builds confidence

Welcome to Brightpath Maths Tutoring. We help students in Grades 6 to 12 understand maths, not just memorise it. Our lessons are friendly, patient and planned around each student.

Why families choose us

  • Lessons planned around each student’s level and goals
  • Online or in-person sessions
  • Clear progress notes after every lesson
  • A free sample worksheet to try before you book

How it works

  1. Send us your application using the student application form.
  2. We contact you within two working days to discuss goals.
  3. Your student takes a free 30-minute trial lesson.
  4. You choose a weekly schedule that suits your family.
