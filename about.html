Our story

Brightpath began with a simple idea: every student can enjoy maths when it is explained clearly. Our tutor is a classroom teacher who saw how many learners fall behind because of one missed idea, not a lack of ability.

Today we teach online and in person, in small groups and one to one.

Our approach

Patient
We explain an idea as many times and in as many ways as needed.
Practical
We use real examples, such as shopping, sports and travel, to make numbers meaningful.
Progress-focused
Every lesson ends with a short summary so families can see what was learned.
