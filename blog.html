Three ways to make fractions feel easy

14 September 2026

Fractions confuse many students because they look like two numbers at once. Try these ideas:

  1. Draw it. Sketch a pizza or a bar and shade the parts.
  2. Use real objects. Cut an apple into equal pieces.
  3. Compare with a number line. See which fraction is bigger.

Calm exam preparation in four steps

2 November 2026

  1. List the topics in the exam.
  2. Practise a little every day, not all at once.
  3. Mark your own work and study your mistakes.
  4. Sleep well the night before.
