Frequently asked questions

Do you offer a trial lesson?

Yes. The first 30-minute lesson is free so your student can meet the tutor.

Can lessons take place online?

Yes. Online lessons use a video call and a shared digital whiteboard.

What if we need to cancel?

Please tell us at least 24 hours before the lesson, and we will rebook it for free.

How to book

  1. Fill in the application form.
  2. Wait for our reply by email within two working days.
  3. Attend your free trial lesson.
